MUSICALMISHAP

Everyone gets two minutes to write a banger. Then you all vote.

or

How it works
  1. Everyone gets the same silly theme, like a duck with a briefcase.
  2. You get two minutes in the studio. Drums, bass, melody. Notes are locked to the key, so nothing sounds truly awful.
  3. All the tracks play back one at a time. Everyone rates each one out of five — except your own.
  4. Highest total after the last round wins.
